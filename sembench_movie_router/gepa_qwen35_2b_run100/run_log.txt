1. **Explicitness**: Sentiment indicators are direct, unambiguous, and grammatically straightforward (e.g., "I loved it," "This is terrible").

### Criteria for "mini" Routing:
Select "mini" in any scenario where the review contains elements that challenge Nano's pattern matching capabilities, including but not limited to:
1. **Subtle Implication & Sarcasm**: Sentiment conveyed through irony (e.g., praising a flaw as if it were a feature) or understatement.

3. **Negation & Conditional Logic**: Phrases involving double negatives ("not bad"), hypotheticals ("would be better if..."), or conditional outcomes that invert sentiment expectations.
4. **Cultural References**: Allusions to specific movies, directors, franchises (e.g., Marvel Cinematic Universe), or industry tropes requiring external knowledge to interpret correctly.
5. **Compressed/Critic Language**: Use of jargon ("forehead-slapping," "showmanship") that implies a negative connotation in this context but might be misinterpreted literally by Nano.
6. **Ambiguous Targets**: Unclear whether the review is about the film, an actor's performance, or a specific plot point (e.g., "[Jackman] captures...").
